Minor 9 is the safest "instant color" move — a 9th above a m7 chord clashes with nothing, so it always sounds smooth. Dominant 9 (built on this key's own dominant) gives the strongest pull back to the tonic. For 11ths and 13ths, switch to Dorian: natural minor's ♭13 sits a half-step above the 5th and clashes — Dorian's natural 13 removes that clash, so 11/13 voicings sound clean instead of muddy.
