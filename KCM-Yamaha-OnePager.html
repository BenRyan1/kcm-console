Every music education platform teaches notes. KCM makes music visible — as a unified, real-time color language that maps pitch, harmony, and emotion into a single perceptual system. It is the only platform that combines polyphonic chord detection from live audio with a proprietary chromatic color grammar, synchronized across piano, guitar, staff notation, and ear training — all in the browser, zero install.
The Three Distinctions
🎯

Polyphonic Intelligence

HPS harmonic product spectrum + 12-bin chroma vectors + cosine similarity chord matching. Detects full chords from mic input in real time — not just single pitches. 20 chord types, 5-frame stability gate, broadcasts to all panels simultaneously via KCM.bus.

🎨

12-Color Chromatic Spectrum™

A proprietary perceptual color grammar: each of the 12 pitch classes has a fixed, full-saturation color. C = Yellow at 12 o'clock, clockwise to B. Students see chord relationships, key signatures, and modal structure as color patterns — leveraging spatial and chromatic memory simultaneously.

🔗

Cross-Panel Visual Sync

A single event bus (KCM.bus) drives 8+ synchronized panels: Chromatic Circle, Piano Keyboard, Guitar Fretboard, Staff Notation, Ear Training, Modal Stencil, Circle of Fifths, and Mic Detection. One detected chord lights up every representation at once.
